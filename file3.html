File 3

In this file we practise talking about things around us, asking questions and giving simple information in English.

3A — Things around you

Vocabulary · Grammar · Pronunciation

📖 Vocabulary

Useful everyday objects:

book
phone
bag
key
pen
table
chair
computer

🔤 Grammar

Practise using this and that to talk about objects.

Example: This is a book.

Example: That is a chair.

🗣 Pronunciation

Practise saying short sentences clearly and paying attention to word stress.

3B — Questions and answers

Grammar · Speaking · Listening

🔤 Grammar

Practise simple questions and short answers.

Example: Is this your phone?

Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

💬 Speaking

Ask and answer questions about objects around you.

🎧 Listening

Listen for key words and identify objects mentioned in a short conversation.

3C — Describe your world

Vocabulary · Speaking · Writing

📖 Vocabulary

Practise describing simple objects and places.

big
small
new
old
good
nice

💬 Speaking

Describe three things in your room using simple English sentences.

✍️ Writing

Write five short sentences about things you have around you.
