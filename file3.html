3A — What's in your bag?

Grammar: singular and plural nouns + a / an
Vocabulary: things you can find in a bag

🔊 Vocabulary

book — книга
pen — ручка
pencil — карандаш
key — ключ
watch — часы
phone — телефон

Grammar

We use a before a consonant sound: a book, a pen.

We use an before a vowel sound: an umbrella.

Choose the correct answer:

1. ___ book

2. ___ umbrella

3. ___ pen

3B — Is that a hat?

Grammar: this / that / these / those
Vocabulary: souvenirs and things you can buy or see

🎧 Phrase Practice

Is that a hat?
Is this a postcard?
What is that?

Grammar

This = one thing near you.

That = one thing farther away.

These = several things near you.

Those = several things farther away.

Choose the correct word:

1. ___ is my hat.

2. ___ are my postcards.

3. ___ is your mug over there.
