What's in your bag?

📚 Vocabulary

book — книга
pen — ручка
pencil — карандаш
bag — сумка
phone — телефон
key — ключ

📖 Grammar

A / AN

We use a before a consonant sound:

a book
a pen
a bag

We use an before a vowel sound:

an apple
an orange
